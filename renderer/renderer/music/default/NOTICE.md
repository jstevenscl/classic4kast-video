These 10 background music tracks are vendored from
[netbymatt/ws4kp-music](https://github.com/netbymatt/ws4kp-music), the
official companion repo to [netbymatt/ws4kp](https://github.com/netbymatt/ws4kp)
(MIT licensed -- same reuse basis as `renderer/fonts/` and
`renderer/icons_all/`). Per that repo's README, these are AI-generated
(Suno.ai) tracks styled after 1990s Weather Channel music -- not recreations
or rips of any specific copyrighted recording. Safe to include in a public
release.

This is the "public-safe" music directory -- see `renderer/music/extended/`
for a second, EDM-private-only set with less certain provenance that must
NOT ship in any future public/standalone release of this renderer.
